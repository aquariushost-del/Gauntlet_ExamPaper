## Why Drive does not hold the full DOCX/PDF binaries
The Google Drive MCP `create_file` path used by this agent cannot inline-upload ~40–800 KB binaries (base64 payload limit ~18 KB). Earlier markdown-only uploads and later “slim” DOCX stubs were incomplete (figures stripped). Those stubs were trashed.
