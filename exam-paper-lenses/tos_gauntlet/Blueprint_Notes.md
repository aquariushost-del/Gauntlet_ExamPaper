## Scope and syllabus authority

- Syllabus file: `5086_Physics_Key_Syllabus_2026.md`
- **In scope:** 11(f) action of a thin converging lens on a light beam; 11(g) define focal length; 11(h) describe images as real/virtual, magnified/diminished and upright/inverted **from supplied evidence**.
- **Explicit exclusions:** recall of image characteristics for specific object distances; thin-lens equation \(1/u+1/v=1/f\); Pure Physics-only TIR/critical-angle calculations; diverging-lens case tables; motor/EM content.
- Practical link: syllabus investigation 8 (position and characteristics of images formed by a thin converging lens) and listed apparatus (converging lens f ≈ 15 cm, lens holder, screen, torch, rules).
