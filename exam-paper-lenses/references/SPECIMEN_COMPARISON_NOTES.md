Reference: `specimen_5086_P5.pdf`. Physics Q1 occupies printed pages 2–7 and totals 15 marks. Chemistry Q2 is on pp.8–10; page 11 is blank; Chemistry notes occupy page 12. P5 original p.6 equation is `gradient = π²K/g`, with `g = 981 cm/s²`. Text extraction corrupts π and fraction layout. Visual inspection confirms it. Here t is the time for ten oscillations, so do not silently replace the supplied relation with a familiar single-period formula. A new assessment can use another supplied relationship, but must define quantities and independently verify it.

## Diagram acceptance evidence for future projects

Compare against relevant reference classes: instrument face to P1 Q1; complex everyday machinery to P1 Q5; piston to P1 Q8; wavefront geometry to P1 Q9; circuits to P1 Q14/15; option arrows to P1 Q18/19; actual laboratory assembly to P5 Fig.1.1. Existing P2 figures provide additional classes. For an absent context choose the closest illustration class and explain the choice.

Use image generation when it yields better original illustration of complex apparatus or everyday objects. Verify functional details and overlay exact measurements, pointers, readouts, axes, circuits and labels. Avoid false assurance from either photorealistic generated artwork or clean but crude vector primitives. The target is the relevant specimen's professional technical drawing standard.
