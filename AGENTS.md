This project creates Physics P1 MCQs, P2 structured papers, Physics P5 practicals and mixed revision packages using exactly two roles and two review loops.

## Two roles and two loops

Builder AI creates the TOS. A genuinely separate Independent Critic AI reviews it before any question drafting. Run up to three TOS rounds; drafting is blocked until all mandatory blueprint checks pass.

Builder then creates numbered paper drafts and Critic independently solves every item, checks all options where applicable, examines every figure and rendered page, compares with the selected specimen and records evidence. Use genuine separate agents where supported. Run up to five full-paper rounds; exceed the limit only on explicit teacher instruction. Keep all drafts and reports. Never invent reviews, scores, pilot results or checks.

After paper acceptance, Builder creates the full answer scheme without a third Gauntlet loop. If the paper does not pass but the teacher explicitly requests the scheme, provide it as a draft with honest status.

## Mandatory profile checks

Read master §14 and record `PAPER_PROFILE`, scope, reference pages and the visual plan before the TOS.

- **P1:** Default at least 40% figure-based MCQs, including at least 30% of all MCQs requiring essential information extraction from the visual. For 40 MCQs this is 16 figure-based including 12 essential-reading items; for 20 it is 8 including 6. These are school defaults, not official SEAB quotas; document explicit teacher exceptions. Context-only pictures and prose describing absent graphs do not satisfy essential-reading counts. Audit visual coverage separately from P2. Include actual instrument/graph reading where the assessed scope calls for it. Verify one defensible answer, plausible distractors, all four options and absence of answer-pattern/visual cues. Match compact specimen pagination and A–D option layouts; never force each MCQ onto a new page or add P2 answer spaces.
- **P2:** Retain specimen-measured answer spaces, indents, part hierarchy, calculation-line geometry and bracket placement. Start each top-level structured question on a new page. Numerical answer labels are right-aligned so the equals sign ends at one fixed x; use dot-leader tabs to the unit/bracket. Qualitative marks end the last dotted line; labelled groups and drawn answers have their mark bracket below, as measured from the original P2 PDF. Context and command paragraphs follow master §9.
- **P5 Physics:** Build an executable investigation with professional apparatus drawings, stated variables, realistic ranges/resolutions, repeats, results table, graph grid, processing and evaluation/extension. Start an investigation on a new page, then allow coherent continuations. Measure procedural and graph layouts from P5, not P2. Distinguish total oscillation timing from period, measured from calculated values, and illustrative data from actual pilot results. Check technician/setup needs and physical feasibility; disclose any unperformed pilot. Do not claim a proposed setup is an official confidential instruction.
- **Mixed revision:** Apply the correct profile separately to each section and preserve the teacher's topic boundary/counts. A good P2 figure cannot compensate for a text-only P1 section.

## Stricter diagrams

Diagram quality has **35% weight**. Require **at least 99/100 for the diagram category**, **98/100 for every individual figure**, and **96/100 for every figure subcriterion**. Overall acceptance remains **99/100**, all other categories at least **96/100**, with zero unresolved critical or major diagram defects and all mandatory checks verified. The worst-figure gate cannot be averaged away.

For every figure, including every MCQ diagram option, require a relevant specimen crop beside the produced figure at comparable physical size. Inspect the final-size greyscale page and readable figure crop, not only a contact sheet or source file. Recognisability alone is insufficient. Rougher-than-specimen illustrations are capped at 95; crude placeholders or distorted apparatus are major defects capped at 89; unresolved visible cosmetic defects cap a figure at 97. Wrong scientific geometry/readouts are critical and block release.

Use image generation when it can make complex objects, people or apparatus closer to specimen-quality technical illustration. Do not approve a crude deterministic sketch merely because it is easy to draw. Inspect and correct generated artwork, and add precise scales, digits, circuit connections, graph geometry, dimensions, arrows and labels as verified overlays wherever practical. Retain source assets. Image generation does not establish scientific correctness; equally, a high-resolution vector is not automatically a professional drawing. Simple scientific schematics remain appropriate when that is the representation in the matching specimen.

## Mark demand and visible answer lines

Apply master §§8.1–8.2 and §9.7 as mandatory gates. Record every structured part's distinct credit points, shortest valid solution, already-credited dependencies and actual specimen comparator. Do not give an automatic method-plus-answer pair for a single arithmetic or substitution step, and never inflate marks to reach a total. Reapprove a changed TOS before revising questions. If tab leaders disappear in the teacher's viewer, use robust editable dots, inspect every response area and disclose untested viewers.

## Output and honesty

Deliver editable Word `Final_TOS.docx`, `Final_Exam_Paper.docx` and `Final_Answer_Scheme.docx`, the quality report, and both loops' draft/review trail. For P5 include proposed technician/setup information as appropriate. Preserve figure sources and comparison evidence.

Save each assessment project to its own subfolder of the designated Google Drive parent in master §11.1. This instruction authorises routine folder creation and uploads there. Verify file names and parents; never change sharing without instruction. If delivery fails, provide actual files and say what remains incomplete.

Do not declare readiness without meeting the applicable gates. Disclose unperformed XSD validation, non-Word rendering, untested Word pagination and physical printing/pilots; report the tightest page feet. Preserve the existing evidence and round-limit rules. Diagram quality must be judged against the original specimen, not the Builder's claims.

## Student notes

When the teacher asks for student notes on any physics or science topic, follow master §15. Deliver a colourful, student-friendly DOCX plus PDF with an SIO box in every section, worked examples and high-quality diagrams. Cover: St Anthony's Canossian Secondary School; use the syllabus code, SIOs and syllabus source document for the subject/topic the teacher gives (e.g. K326 Physics); say "SEC exam"; no stats block; modest decoration. No internal labels such as "verbatim" and no duplicate syllabus-outcome lines. House colour style (v1.7.1): all shaded elements (banners, heading bars, SIO boxes, worked-example and table headers, callouts, cover art) use soft pastel fills with dark navy text (e.g. #14213D), never white text on dark fills. Make everything greyscale-safe (labels, not colour alone). Keep headings with next; no blank gap over ~130 pt except the cover. Labels ≥ 9 pt, rasters ≥ 600 ppi; diagrams scientifically accurate, using standard conventions, with natural anatomy (e.g. field lines never cross; Fleming's left hand with mutually perpendicular fingers); trace any supplied photo. Render every page to PNG and inspect every figure. Builder Draft_NN → Critic (target 99); teacher may accept directly. Keep only FINAL docx/pdf in the project folder; move everything else into `Older drafts` in the Drive Archive folder (move, never trash). Never email teachers directly.
